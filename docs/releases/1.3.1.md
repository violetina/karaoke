# Karaoke Platform 1.3.1

A patch release delivering a critical timing fix for stage/kiosk playback, along with expanded visual art and matching rules for all extended mood classifications.

## Playback & Stage Views

- **Song Position vs. Queue Position**: Fixed an issue where `/tv` and the kiosk stage view showed accumulated session elapsed time instead of song position. Because YouTube Music streams an entire queue through a single `<video>` element, its `currentTime` and `duration` count continuously across multiple songs. Playback inspection in `player_open._PLAYBACK_JS` now queries `#movie_player`'s `getCurrentTime()` and `getDuration()`, aligning kiosk reporting with MPRIS to the tenth of a second, with automatic fallback on generic HTML5 video pages.

## Mood Visuals & Classifications

- **Extended Feeling Pixel Art**: Added 44 new 64×64 pixel art illustrations covering all 19 extended feelings introduced in v2 (`festive`, `dreamy`, `romantic`, `melancholic`, `frustrated`, `energetic`, `calm`, `tense`, `bittersweet`, `hopeful`, `cheerful`, `defiant`, `blue`, `uplifting`, `intense`, `dramatic`, `wistful`, `longing`, `ironic`).
- **Dynamic Variation Rotation**: Every feeling and quadrant mood now carries multiple scenes (`1.png`, `2.png`), and both `/api/mood-feeling-image` and `/api/mood-art` randomly cycle through available scenes upon transitions.
- **Graceful Fallbacks**: Added `FEELING_FALLBACKS` in the control API to resolve extended nuances to their parent quadrant mood before defaulting to neutral.
- **Color Target Profiles**: Extended `moodart.MOOD_TARGETS` with calibrated hue, saturation, value, and contrast profiles across all nuanced feelings, ensuring accurate cached cover-art scoring and gradient synthesis.

## Backfill Tooling

- **Chord Timeline Backfill**: Added `scripts/backfill_chords.py` with multi-threaded detection and OpenSearch harmonic indexing, backfilling v2 chords across local library tracks.
- **Mood Timeline Backfill**: Updated `scripts/backfill_moods.py` and regenerated v2 mood timelines to incorporate the latest chord progression and blues harmony signals.

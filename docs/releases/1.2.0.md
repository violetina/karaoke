# Karaoke Platform 1.2.0

A release about **mood** and **the radio**, and about making the
post-processing pipeline keep up with both. Mood timelines become feelings with
swings and borderliners, the graph follows the radio and collects songs into
playlists, every song the radio hears joins the library fully processed, and
Whisper lyric sync moves onto its own queue so it no longer stalls everything
behind it.

## Mood

- **Mood timelines** (#96): swings and borderliners, scored from sound
  (arousal) and lyric meaning (valence), and shown on `/graph` (#97).
- **Feelings** (#106): frustrated, longing, dreamy, festive and more, plus the
  contrast between what the sound says and what the words say.
- **No more loudness-as-anger** on `/mood` (#95). Each segment is named by its
  own average (#105).
- `/mood` and the stage views follow radio mode and recording sessions (#89).
- Faster: prompt texts embedded once per process (#108), each cached cover
  decoded once (#116). A corrupt audio file is skipped instead of ending the
  backfill (#102).

## Radio and graph

- Every song the radio hears joins the library, fully processed (#92). A
  missing mood timeline takes the full chain (#99); radio captures are no
  longer analysed as tracks (#98).
- `/graph` locates the radio song, finds songs with songrec (#90), follows the
  radio and the desktop player, and places unanalysed songs (#91).
- **Collector** on `/graph`: gather songs, save them as a playlist, play them
  as a queue (#100).
- songrec scans are planned from the song's length and listen for its end
  (#107).

## DJ, playlists and MCP

- DJ suggestions by fit (sound and mood handover), with key and tempo as
  constraints (#111).
- Refreshable `Genre:` and `Smart:` playlists (#115).
- MCP: chord-progression search, track chords and playlist listing (#119),
  strict chord matching (#121), `play_playlist`, and the default DJ list
  resolved by name (#120).
- TUI: **A** follows the radio ingest, **k** hands off to it, **P** redoes
  chords (#112).

## Post-processing pipeline

- **Dedicated Whisper sync queue** (#122): `sync_lyrics` runs on
  `karaoke-sync` with its own worker, `karaoke-celery-sync.service`
  (2 processes x 3 threads). A 10-40 minute sync no longer holds up the fast
  steps of the next song. The Whisper model is cached per process, and the
  main worker runs 4 processes, recycled every 50 tasks.
- **CLAP in the pipeline** on its own one-process worker (#93), with its own
  routing key (#94).
- One chain per song at a time (#109), now claimed when the song is queued
  (#122). A sync that cannot succeed is remembered (#110), Whisper sync is
  acknowledged on receipt (#114), and it re-checks for synced lyrics before
  transcribing (#122).
- Audio is fetched again if the cache pruned it mid-chain (#101), and is only
  reused or returned once it reads as audio (#104).

## Upgrading

Run `make systemd-install` to link the new `karaoke-celery-sync.service`, then
restart `karaoke.target`. Sync jobs already waiting on
`karaoke-postprocess-celery` still run there until it drains.

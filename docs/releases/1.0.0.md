# Karaoke Platform 1.0.0

A milestone release. 1.0.0 delivers the **Interactive Knowledge & Similarity Graph**, completes the **full-library multi-vector enrichment** across 16,600+ tracks, adds **gypsy jazz and extended chord vocabularies**, and unites multi-modal track similarity across acoustic timbre, chord progressions, lyric sentiment, and Camelot wheel harmonic mixing.

## Interactive Knowledge & Similarity Graph (`graph_view.py`)

- **Interactive Constellation View (`/graph`)**: Web-based Cytoscape.js interface for exploring harmonic, acoustic, and mood relationships across the library. Tracks form nodes colour-coded by Camelot harmonic key or lyric mood, connected by multi-hop similarity edges.
- **Multi-Modal Similarity Strategies**:
  - `structural`: Connects songs based on micro chord motion progressions and circle-of-fifths root travel.
  - `timbre`: Connects songs by acoustic texture using 512-dimensional LAION-CLAP vectors.
  - `mood`: Connects songs by lyrical sentiment and emotional valence.
  - `harmonic`: Connects songs based on Camelot wheel mixing compatibility (relative keys, dominant/subdominant steps).
- **Playlist & Seed-Centric Traversal**: Build graphs centered on single tracks or entire saved playlists (e.g. YouTube Music or Spotify synced playlists), discovering kindred tracks across configurable hops and branching limits.
- **JSON API Endpoints**: `/api/graph/similarity` powers real-time graph traversal for MCP agents, Obot, Antigravity, and headless DJ automations.

## Extended Harmonic Vocabulary (`chords.py`)

- **Gypsy Jazz & Swing Tonics**: Added template support for minor 6th (`m6`), major 6th (`6`), and `6/9` chords. In Django Reinhardt style and gypsy jazz, minor chords typically function as tonic `m6` rather than minor triads or `m7`, and major resolutions favor `6/9`.
- **Dominant 9th Chords (`9`)**: Extended chord detection beyond basic 7th triads to capture 9th chords `(0, 4, 7, 10, 2)` prevalent in funk, blues, and swing.
- **Circle-of-Fifths Root Motion**: Collapsed chord frames into pitch-class transitions, building 12-dimensional root motion histograms invariant to key transposition.

  - Multi-process worker pool capped to safe thread boundaries to prevent OpenSearch connection starvation.
  - Scroll-based pagination (`scroll="2m"`) overcoming OpenSearch's 10,000 document `max_result_window` ceiling during candidate discovery.
  - PostgreSQL failure tracking (`enrichment_failures` table) to isolate unreadable or corrupted audio headers and prevent endless retry loops.
- **Source Resolution Tooling (`scripts/batch_find_sources.py`)**:
  - Batch resolves missing streaming sources for tracks with lyrics while respecting YouTube Music API rate limits.

## Database Deduplication & Merging Optimization (`scripts/db_cleanup.py`)

- **Grouped Deduplication**: Replaced $O(N^2)$ candidate comparisons with normalized artist bucketing, speeding up deduplication passes across 18,000+ tracks.
- **Preserved Multi-Part Titles**: Added `compatible_parts` guard preventing false-positive deduplication of distinct song parts or movements (e.g. Part 1 vs Part 2).
- **Cached Lyrics & Source Scoring**: Pre-fetches source counts and lyric status in bulk, eliminating per-pair SQL roundtrips when choosing canonical records.

## Smart Playlists & Remote Kiosk Playback

- **Acoustic & Mood Matching**: Curated playlist creation powered by multi-vector kindred matching (demonstrated with Kiki Rockwell & Chill Jazz playlists).
- **Direct Playback Control**: Seamless dispatch to Chromium kiosk players via Chrome DevTools Protocol (`try_chrome_cdp_navigate`) and MPRIS integration.
- **Dual-Version Lyrics & Word-Level Sync**: Auto-enqueuing of synced playlist tracks into the Celery postprocessing queue for word-timed LRC upgrades.

# Karaoke Platform 1.1.0

A major harmonic and indexing release. 1.1.0 upgrades **Harmonic Chord Theory (v2)** to 97% ground-truth accuracy, introduces **Live Chord Timeline streaming & visual pop overlay on `/mood`**, resolves the **10,000 OpenSearch scroll window limitation**, protects existing chord vectors via **non-destructive document updates**, and adds the **audited track reprocessor tool (`scripts/reprocess_tracks.py`)**.

## Harmonic Chord Theory v2 (`chords.py` & `harmony.py`)

- **Ground-Truth Benchmarked Accuracy (97% Exact / 100% Root & Family)**:
  - Replaces raw template scoring with a multi-stage harmonic model: picks root and basic triad first, removes overtone leakage, disambiguates same-note inversions via the bass register, smooths transitions using Viterbi state decoding, and resolves extended intervals (7ths, 6ths, 9ths) against the residual energy floor.
  - **Bass-Assisted Inversion Disambiguation**: Resolves ambiguous pitch collections (e.g. C6 vs. Am7, Cm6 vs. Am7b5, or 4-way dim7 permutations) by tracking the fundamental bass octave ($C_1$ to $B_3$).
  - **Overtone Whitening**: Cancels 3rd and 5th harmonic overtone spillover before template evaluation, eliminating false `maj7` and `sus4` over-reporting on dense audio records.
- **Advanced Harmonic Analysis (`harmony.py`)**:
  - Calculates functional Roman numeral representations relative to the detected key.
  - Detects functional cadences: major and minor ii–V–I progressions, tritone substitutions, secondary dominants, Andalusian descents ($i - \flat VII - \flat VI - V$), backdoor cadences, and blues scores.
  - **Fifth Chains (`fifth_chains_per_minute`)**: Quantifies consecutive descending-fifth root motions (rock $\approx 0.24$, blues $\approx 0.32$, jazz $\approx 0.63$ per minute) to classify musical styles resiliently even in noisy audio.

## Live Chord Overlay & Timeline Streaming (`chord_timeline.py` & `stage_view.py`)

- **Live `/mood` Visual Overlay**: Renders real-time chord symbols, active Roman numerals, and upcoming chord predictions directly onto the dynamic stage canvas with an animated pop transition on every chord change.
- **Timed Chord Persistence (`track_chords` table)**: Persists segment-by-segment chord timelines with millisecond start/end timestamps and Roman numeral annotations.
- **Audio/Video Duration Verification**: Automatically hides or suppresses the chord overlay if playback duration deviates from the analysed audio file, preventing out-of-sync overlays when streaming alternate video versions with spoken intros.

## Mood Timelines & Sentiment Anger-Bias Fix (`mood_timeline.py` & `sentiment.py`)

- **Sentiment Anger-Bias Elimination (`sentiment.py`)**:
  - Removed microphone loudness as a raw anger heuristic (`rms > 0.04`), which previously caused 76% of simulated lines to read as angry due to background speaker audio on room microphones. Angry classification dropped from 76% to 2% across library benchmarks.
  - Removed non-anger lyric terms (`fire`, `burn`, `stop`, `break`, `ho`) and common Dutch conversational terms (`niet`, `wel`, `nee`, `klopt`) that previously skewed captions toward anger.
  - Ties now default to `neutral` instead of angry-first.
- **Dynamic Mood Timelines (`mood_timeline.py`)**:
  - Scores tracks over rolling 10-second windows mapped onto the valence $\times$ arousal plane.
  - Arousal derived from calibrated CLAP embeddings + track-relative loudness dynamics (dB against track median).
  - Valence derived from lyric sentence embeddings vs tone prompts (instrumentals fall back to audio CLAP embeddings).
  - 3-window moving average, hysteresis, and 30-second minimum segment constraint prevent jitter.
  - Borderline segments within 0.15 of axes are reported as dual moods (e.g. `tender <-> happy`).
  - Identifies structural mood swings (e.g. *The Pretender*'s quiet intro transitioning to angry at 0:40; *Moya*'s crescendo).
  - Full pipeline integration: `mood` task executed on the dedicated CLAP worker (`karaoke-celery-clap.service`), persisting to `track_moods` table.

## OpenSearch 10k Scroll & Safe Re-Store

- **Scan Pagination (`scripts/clap_index.py` & `scripts/reprocess_tracks.py`)**:
  - Replaced fixed `size: 10000` searches with `opensearchpy.helpers.scan(...)` across `karaoke-clap` and `karaoke-progression`, preventing tracks past document #10,000 from being falsely identified as missing.
- **Non-Destructive Key Progression Upsert (`key_progression.py`)**:
  - Switched `key_progression.store` from destructive full-document replacement to partial updates (`client.update` with `doc_as_upsert: True`).
  - Re-running key/BPM analysis on an existing track no longer erases previously detected `chord_motion` vectors if chord detection is skipped or fails on an alternate recording.

## Audited Track Reprocessor Tool (`scripts/reprocess_tracks.py`)

- **Audited Decoding Pipeline**:
  - Automatically identifies and handles common audio flaws: mp3s wrapped in RIFF/WAV headers with ID3 tags, truncated audio files, video-edit intro offsets, and silence/corrupted streams ($< -50$ dB).
  - Decodes once to a normalized temporary WAV file and runs all pending pipeline stages (Key/BPM, Harmony, CLAP timbre vector, Whisper plain-lyric sync, OpenSearch document indexing).
  - Produces structured CSV audit reports with classification statuses (`ok`, `suspect`, `broken`, `missing`).
